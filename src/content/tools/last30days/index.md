last30days is a research skill that answers "what is actually happening with this right now?" Give it a topic, a person or a company and it searches the places where people react in real time, such as Reddit threads, X posts, YouTube transcripts, Hacker News discussions and prediction markets, in parallel. It then writes a summary weighted by real engagement rather than by search ranking.

The premise is that no single model or search engine sees all of these sources, and the freshest signal lives in comments and posts, not in articles. The brief cites what it found, so you can follow up on the threads that matter.

**When to use it:** before a meeting or a sales call, catching up on a fast-moving topic, checking community sentiment on a product or library before you adopt it, or picking up techniques newer than your model's training data.

**Caveats:** results are only as good as the conversation, so niche topics can come back thin. The core sources work without setup; others need extra configuration or API keys. It reports what people are saying, which is not the same as verified fact.

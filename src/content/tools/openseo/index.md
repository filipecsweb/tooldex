OpenSEO is an SEO suite for people who find Semrush or Ahrefs too expensive or too sprawling. It covers the core workflows, such as keyword research, rank tracking, competitor insights, backlinks, site audits and visibility in AI answers, in a simpler interface. You pay for the search data you use rather than for a big suite's seats.

It's also built for agents. An MCP server exposes your SEO data to Claude Code and other agents, and ready-made agent skills walk the agent through tasks like keyword research or a competitor landscape. You can use the hosted version or self-host it, and fork it to shape the tool around your own process.

**When to use it:** small teams and solo founders who need real SEO data without an enterprise contract, or anyone who wants their agent to research keywords and audit a site from the chat.

**Caveats:** SEO data comes from DataForSEO and is paid for by use: self-hosting needs your own DataForSEO key, and the hosted plan bills that usage for you. Self-hosting takes some setup. It deliberately covers focused workflows rather than everything the big suites do.

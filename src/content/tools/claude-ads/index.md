Claude Ads turns Claude Code into an assistant for paid advertising work. Point it at exports or authorised reads from your ad accounts, across the major search, social, video and retail platforms, and it produces audits with dated evidence and stated confidence, campaign and budget plans, creative briefs, experiment designs and client-ready reports.

What sets it apart is how cautious it is. It's read-only by default: changes to live accounts are drafted, not applied, and only go through when the specific platform and operation pass explicit approval and verification gates. Results are produced as structured data first and rendered second, so runs are reproducible and comparable over time.

**When to use it:** you run paid media for clients or for your own company and want faster, more consistent audits and reporting without handing an agent the keys to your ad spend.

**Caveats:** it's a big skill with its own command set to learn. Live account access needs credentials per platform, and by design it won't make changes on its own. Claude Code is the primary host; other agent hosts can load the skill files with varying support.

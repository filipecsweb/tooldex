Tinycast is a keyboard launcher for the Mac, in the mould of Spotlight, Alfred or Raycast. One global hotkey opens a palette where you launch apps, search files, run shell commands and Apple Shortcuts, do calculations and conversions, look up words and paste from your clipboard history. Snippets, quicklinks, window management, system actions, notes and your next meeting live in the same place.

It's built natively in SwiftUI and AppKit with no third-party dependencies or telemetry, and it aims to stay light on memory. It can run Raycast extensions you already use and import a Raycast setup. AI features, including a chat window and quick actions that rewrite, translate or summarise selected text, are off by default and use your own key or an installed AI account.

**When to use it:** you want a fast, free, open-source launcher on macOS, or you use Raycast and want a native alternative that keeps your extensions.

**Caveats:** it's a general Mac productivity app rather than agent tooling; the AI features are one part of it, not the point. It's macOS only and targets recent versions. Raycast extension support may not cover every extension.
